Welcome

A calm stay in the land of Omkar

In Omkareshwar, the holy Narmada flows around Mandhata island, which is shaped like the sacred Om. The island is home to one of the twelve Jyotirlingas. Hotel Shree Shivay is a short walk from the temples and the food street, so you can go for early morning darshan and come back to a clean, cool room to rest.

Our rooms are new, with marble-finish floors, warm cove lighting, designer headboards and attached bathrooms with hot water. The owner and staff are always happy to guide you on darshan, boating and local sightseeing.

Darshan and more, close to your room

Walking times are from Google Maps. Ask us for the best darshan timings on the day of your visit.

  • 550 m · 7 min walk

    Shri Mamleshwar Jyotirlinga

    The ancient temple on the southern bank of the Narmada. Pilgrims visit both Mamleshwar and Omkareshwar for a complete darshan.

  • 900 m · 17 min walk

    Shri Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga

    One of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, on Mandhata island in the middle of the Narmada.

    The sacred walk around Mandhata island, with temples and river views along the way.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How far is the hotel from the temples?

Shri Mamleshwar Jyotirlinga is about 550 m away (a 7-minute walk). Shri Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga is about 900 m away (a 17-minute walk). The food street is also nearby.

How much does a room cost?

Rooms start from ₹2,050 per night. The rate depends on your dates, festival season and AC or Non-AC room. Send us a WhatsApp message for the exact rate.

How do I book a room?

Send us a WhatsApp message on 88279 95348 or call 88279 95348 / 62666 90461. Tell us your dates, number of guests and whether you want AC or Non-AC.

What is the check-in time?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM. If you are arriving early, message us and we will let you know what is possible.

Is parking available?

Yes, parking is available for guests. Let us know your vehicle when you book.

Do the rooms have Wi-Fi and hot water?

Yes. Wi-Fi is free for guests, and our attached bathrooms have a geyser for hot water.
